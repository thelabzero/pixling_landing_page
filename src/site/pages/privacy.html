1 · Scope

This policy explains how Pixling ("we", "us") handles personal data when you visit this website or reserve a Dino. It follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act"). It covers this website only. The Dino device has no camera and no microphone.

2 · What we collect

Information you give us. When you reserve a Dino we ask for your name and email address, and, only if you choose to give it, your mobile number.

Information collected as you use the site.

  • Usage events, such as which sections of the page you view and whether you start or submit the form.
  • Technical details your browser sends, such as device type, browser, screen size, language and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Session recordings of how a visit unfolds (clicks, taps and scrolling). Anything you type into a form field is masked and is not recorded.

We do not collect payment details on this site. Pre-orders have not opened, and nothing is charged when you reserve.

3 · How we use it

  • To tell you about Dino pre-orders: when they open, the price and the shipping date. This is the only reason we use your name, email address and mobile number.
  • To improve the website: usage events and session recordings help us find what is confusing or broken.
  • To keep the site working and secure.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for advertising and we do not share it so that others can market to you.

4 · Consent

We process your reservation details because you gave them to us and asked to be told about pre-orders. By submitting the form you consent to this use. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 10) and withdrawing is as easy as giving it. After you withdraw, we stop contacting you and delete your details, unless the law requires us to keep them.

5 · Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers who process it on our behalf, and only for the purposes above:

  • PostHog, our analytics and reservation-capture provider, which receives usage events, session recordings and the details you submit in the form.
  • Hosting providers that serve this website and so see technical request data such as IP addresses.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, for example in response to a lawful request from a court or authority.

6 · Where it is processed

We use PostHog's European region, so your data is processed outside India. The DPDP Act permits this except to countries the Government of India has restricted. We will review this if that changes.

7 · Cookies and local storage

This site stores small pieces of information in your browser:

  • An analytics identifier (cookie and local storage), so that a return visit is recognised as the same visitor.
  • A local-storage note that you have already reserved a Dino, so the form can show your confirmation instead of asking again.

You can clear these at any time in your browser settings, or block them. The site still works, though the analytics will not recognise a return visit.

8 · How long we keep it

We keep your reservation details until pre-orders have opened and we have told you, or until you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first. Usage events and session recordings are kept only as long as they are useful for improving the site, and then deleted.

9 · Security

We take reasonable security safeguards to protect personal data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) to this site, masking of form fields in session recordings, and limiting access to the data to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

10 · Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct or complete it;
  • ask us to erase it;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • get your grievance addressed (section 12); and
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to.

To use any of them, write to {{email}}. We may ask you to confirm it is you before we act, and we aim to reply within 30 days.

11 · Children

This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and think a child has given us their details, write to us and we will delete them.

12 · Contact and grievances

For questions about this policy, requests about your data, or complaints, write to {{email}}. If we do not resolve your complaint, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

13 · Changes

We may update this policy as the site or the law changes. We will change the date at the top, and where a change affects how we use your data we will tell you before it applies.
