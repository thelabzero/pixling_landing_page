01 · the idea

What is a Pixling?

A Pixling is a character made of pixels. It has no body to speak of and no room to live in. Everything it feels, it shows on its face: a few squares that blink, peek and get sleepy.

We think the simplest unit of a screen is enough to carry a whole personality. A pixel is small, honest and a little funny. So that is where every Pixling starts, as a single pixel that wakes up, stands up and becomes someone.

Meet the first one

Dino is a small desk dinosaur with two slab eyes, brow ridges and one fang. It lives on a 128 × 64 screen set into a palm-sized printed body. It tells the time, keeps you company while you focus, and does a tiny rawr every few minutes, just for you.

02 · what we believe

Four promises

01Simple

One tap, one clear thing. It works out of the box: no account, no setup to enjoy it.

02Honest

No camera, no microphone, no tracking in the device. It is a companion, not a sensor.

03Made to be kept

Solid in the hand, tough enough for a bag, built to stay on a desk for years, not weeks.

04Alive in pixels

A few glowing squares can carry a whole personality. We take that seriously.
