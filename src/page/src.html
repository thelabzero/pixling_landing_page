Meet Dino

05 · what's inside

Three printed parts. A tiny computer inside.

A body, a screen bezel and a back plate, printed in matte PLA. Inside: everything Dino needs to notice you, and nothing that watches you.

  1. 1
    A 128 × 64 screen1-bit and crisp. Every face and number is drawn pixel by pixel, and the clock shifts a pixel every few minutes to protect the screen.
  2. 2
    TouchTap, double-tap, hold to pet, long press for the menu.
  3. 3
    Motion sensingIt feels tilts, shakes, flips and being picked up.
  4. 4
    Rechargeable batteryDino lets you know when it's running low.
  5. 5
    USB-C chargingThe cable comes in the box.

    No camera, no microphoneNothing that watches or listens. No tracking, no account.
