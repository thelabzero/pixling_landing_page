Questions about Dino.

The honest answers. Where we do not know yet, we say so.

What is Dino?
A small desk friend with a screen for a face. It blinks, gets sleepy, and shows the time when you double-tap it. It is the first Pixling.
What is a Pixling?
A character made of pixels, living on a screen. Dino is the first. Read more.
Does Dino have a camera or microphone?
No. Never.
Does Dino need Wi-Fi?
Yes, to get the time.
Do I need an app or an account?
No account. An optional app is coming, to connect your Dino and explore more. Dino works without it.
What can Dino do?
Show the time, ring an alarm, run a stopwatch, and keep you company while you focus (25, 45 or 60 minutes). It has 24 faces and gets sleepy if left alone.
How do I use it?
Touch. Tap, double-tap, hold to pet, long press for the menu.
How big is it?
About 7.5 cm tall. Three printed parts in matte PLA.
How do I charge it?
USB-C. The cable is in the box.
How long does the battery last?
About 8 hours in normal mode, and about 12 hours in Power Saving mode.
What does it cost and when does it ship?
Not announced yet. Reserve a Dino and we will tell you first.
Does reserving cost anything?
No. Nothing is charged today.
What colours are there?
Matte black first. More later.
Games and friends?
Coming in a later update, not in the first release.
What happens to my details?
We only use them to tell you about pre-orders. Privacy policy.

Where an answer is still "not yet", we will fill it in here as soon as we know.
