Questions about Dino.

The honest answers. Where we do not know yet, we say so.

What is Dino?
Dino is a small desk companion with a 128 × 64 screen for a face. It blinks, peeks, gets sleepy, and shows you the time when you double-tap it. It is the first Pixling. See Dino up close.
What is a Pixling?
A Pixling is a character made of pixels, one that lives entirely on a screen. Every Pixling starts as a single pixel that wakes up and becomes someone. Read the idea behind it.
Does Dino have a camera or a microphone?
No. Dino has no camera and no microphone, by design. It is a companion, not a sensor.
Do I need an app or an account?
No. Dino works out of the box, with no account and no setup to enjoy it.
What can Dino do?
It shows the time, and works as an alarm and a stopwatch. It runs focus sessions of 25, 45 or 60 minutes and leads you through a short breathing break afterwards. It has 24 expressions, does a tiny rawr every few minutes, and falls asleep if you leave it alone for five minutes.
How do I control it?
By touch. Tap, double-tap, hold to pet it, or long press for the menu. It also feels tilts, shakes, flips and being picked up.
How big is Dino and what is it made of?
Dino is about 7.5 cm tall. The body, the screen bezel and the back plate are three printed parts in matte PLA.
How do I charge it?
Dino has a rechargeable battery and charges over USB-C. The cable comes in the box. It also lets you know when it is running low.
How long does the battery last?
We have not published a figure yet. We will share a measured number with the spec sheet rather than a guess.
How much does Dino cost, and when does it ship?
Pre-orders have not opened, and the price and shipping date are not announced yet. Reserve a Dino and we will write to you first, with both, when pre-orders open.
What does it cost to reserve one?
Nothing. Reserving only puts you on the list, and nothing is charged today.
What colours does it come in?
Matte black to start. More colours come later.
What are the games and friends features?
Tiny games (meteor dodge, snack snap, desk pong, hot egg) and pairing two Dinos by knocking them together are planned for later updates. They are not in the first release.
What do you do with the details I give?
We use your name, email and optional mobile number only to tell you about Dino pre-orders. Read the privacy page for the full detail.

Where an answer is still "not yet", we will fill it in here as soon as we know.
